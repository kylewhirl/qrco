# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, productive place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and respectful.
- Focus criticism on code, design, documentation, and behavior.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clarity when something is wrong or unclear.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct concerns to `hello@tqrco.de`.
